title: "nuPSX 0.6.0"

---

[nuPSX 0.6.0](https://github.com/maxpoletaev/nupsx/releases/tag/v0.6.0) is out.

Main things I worked on are internal resolution upscaling and a hardware (OpenGL) renderer.
The latter has compatibility issues in some games, but lets you run them at higher
resolutions without FPS drops.

This is also the first release with fixes from someone other than me.
Thanks to [mdmrk](https://github.com/mdmrk) and [PanAeon](https://github.com/PanAeon)!
